Support

How do I play?

You start in the top-left corner. Pick a color and your region takes it, spreading into every matching tile it touches. Keep going until the whole board is one color, in as few moves as you can. Try a Mini board here, or go to Settings › Help › How to play in the app to see the tips again.

What is par?

Par is a strong score worked out for each board by looking a few moves ahead. It isn't the fewest moves possible, so you can beat it, on Classic and Mini alike.

When does the daily board change?

There's a new Daily Classic and Daily Mini at midnight UTC, the same for everyone. You can replay a daily as often as you like that day, and your best goes to the Game Center leaderboard. You can play signed out too; your score is sent if you sign in to Game Center later the same day.

Can I undo a move?

No. Every move counts toward your score, so par and the leaderboards stay fair. Replay starts the same board again.

How do challenges work?

When you solve a board, tap Challenge a friend and send the link. Your friend plays the same board, in the app or in the App Clip without downloading anything, and sees your moves next to theirs once they've solved it.

A challenge link didn't open

Make sure the whole link came through, and that Color Wave is up to date in the App Store, since links from a newer version can't open in an older one. If it still doesn't work, ask your friend to send it again.

Will my history move to a new device?

Yes, if you're signed in to iCloud on both. Your solved boards and settings sync through your own iCloud account.

The colors are hard to tell apart

In Settings, turn on Symbols on tiles to give every color its own shape, or change Color tones and Theme. Color Wave also supports VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion and a left-handed layout.
