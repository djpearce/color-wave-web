Color Wave

Privacy policy

Color Wave doesn't collect any information about you.

Dylan Pearce built Color Wave - Casual Puzzles as a free app, provided at no cost and intended for use as is. This page explains how the app and this website handle your information.

Information collection and use

I do not knowingly collect any information from any users. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send anything to me.

What stays on your devices

Your games, settings and solved boards are saved on your device. If you use iCloud, your history syncs between your devices through your own iCloud account. If you sign in to Game Center, your daily scores go to Apple's leaderboards. Apple handles both under Apple's privacy policy, and I can't see any of it.

A challenge link holds only the board and the sender's moves and time, never a name or anything else about the person who sent it.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. It remembers whether you turned symbols on or off, in your browser only. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Children's privacy

I do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact me and I'll help.

Changes to this privacy policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
